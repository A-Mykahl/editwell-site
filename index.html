How it works

Pick a look
On the Presets tab, tap a pack and then a look. Each tile shows the look on your own photo. Drag Intensity to make it lighter or stronger, and press and hold the photo to compare with the original.
Fine-tune
The Adjust tab has ten sliders: Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Warmth, Tint, Saturation, Fade, Grain and Halation. The line under the list says what the one you last moved does. Double-tap a name to put it back to zero.
Crop
The Crop tab has shapes, rotate, flip and straighten. Pinch to zoom and drag to move the frame. The size the photo will be saved at is shown under the slider.
Save
Tap Save. "Save as New Photo" adds an edited copy to your library and leaves the original as it is. "Edit the Original" changes the photo in your library; Photos keeps the original, and Revert brings it back at any time.
Favourites and your own presets
Long-press a look and choose Add to Favourites; your favourites get a tab of their own. "Save Look as Preset" in the Save menu keeps the look you have tuned, with its adjustments, under My Presets.
Batch Edit
In the photo grid, tap Batch Edit, select photos, choose a look and tap Save Copies. Each photo gets an edited copy; the originals are untouched.
Undo and Reset
Undo steps back one change at a time. Reset clears the look, adjustments and crop; Undo brings them back.

Frequently asked

Where are my photos stored?
In your own photo library, as always. EditWell edits them on your iPhone and never uploads them. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
Do I have to give access to all my photos?
No. You can allow all photos, only the ones you choose, or none. With no library access, EditWell uses Apple's photo picker, which hands the app only the photo you pick. To change your answer later: Settings → Apps → EditWell → Photos, or tap the Photos button in the editor and choose "Allow Access in Settings…".
What do I get for free?
The Essentials pack of eight looks, every adjustment, crop, favourites, your own presets and Batch Edit, with no watermark and no limit.
What are the paid packs?
Signature, Colour Negative and Cinema are packs of eight looks each. Each is a one-time purchase, and Volume 1 is all three together for less. There is no subscription. You can preview any look on your own photos before buying.
I bought a pack and it is locked on my new iPhone.
Open a locked pack, tap Unlock, then "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I edited the original by mistake.
Open the photo in EditWell and tap Revert, or in the Photos app tap Edit → Revert. The original is always kept.
Does it work on iPad or Mac?
EditWell is made for iPhone.

Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Applies to EditWell for iOS, version 1.0 and later.

In short

EditWell does not collect any data. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. Your photos are edited on your iPhone and are never uploaded. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only connection is Apple's App Store, when you look at or buy a preset pack.

Your photos

EditWell reads a photo only to show and edit it on your device, and saves the result to your own photo library. Photos, and anything in them such as location or camera details, never leave your iPhone through EditWell.

You decide what the app can see, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Apps → EditWell → Photos:

Your choiceWhat EditWell can do
Full accessShow your library in its own grid, save edited copies, and edit a photo in place when you ask it to
Limited accessThe same, but only for the photos you selected
Add photos onlySave edited copies. It cannot read your library
NoneNothing in your library. You pick a photo with Apple's photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands over only the photo you choose

A saved copy keeps the original photo's capture date and camera details, as any photo editor does. A copy sent with the Share button has its location removed.

What the app stores on your device

DataWhy
The presets you saved and the looks you marked as favouritesSo they are there next time
The photo you were last editing and its editSo the app reopens where you left off. Replaced when you open another photo
Your settings: light or dark appearance, the pack and tab last usedTo keep the app as you left it

These stay in the app's own storage, protected by iOS, and are included in your normal iPhone backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes all of it. Photos you saved to your library stay in your library.

Purchases

Preset packs are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only which packs you own, so it can unlock them. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment details or purchase history. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't upload, scan or analyse your photos anywhere but on your device.
  • We don't use third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
  • We don't use AI services.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't require or offer an account.
  • We don't send push notifications.

Children

EditWell is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf; all of it is on your device and under your control. If you have questions about this policy you can still reach us below.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, the new version of this policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and the app's release notes will say so.
